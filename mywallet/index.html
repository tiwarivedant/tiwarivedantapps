myWallet

A simple, private budgeting app for iPhone.

Log expenses with an amount, category and date, plus an optional merchant and note. Set a monthly budget for each category, and see how your spending is tracking on the Dashboard. myWallet runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later, and it has no ads.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. myWallet has no server and no account, and it never sends your data anywhere.

Can I use it on more than one device?

No. There's no sync, so your data stays on the iPhone where you entered it.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one. myWallet's data is part of that backup.

How do budgets work from month to month?

A category's budget applies from the month you set it and carries forward to later months until you change it. Changing a budget doesn't affect past months.

What happens if I delete a category?

Its expenses are deleted too, including those from past months, so your History totals will change. This can't be undone.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That permanently removes all of its data.

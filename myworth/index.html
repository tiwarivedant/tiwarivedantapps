myWorth

Your net worth, and your partner's, in one place.

Add your savings, investments, retirement accounts, property and vehicles, along with loans, mortgages and credit cards, and myWorth shows your net worth and how it breaks down. Assign each account to you, your partner or both of you to see totals for each person or combined. You can invite your partner to share the household so you both see and edit the same accounts from your own iPhones. myWorth locks with Face ID or your passcode, runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later, and has no ads.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud account so it's backed up and can be shared. Account names, balances and notes are stored encrypted in iCloud. myWorth has no server and no account of its own, and I can't see your data.

Does myWorth connect to my bank?

No. You enter and update balances yourself. myWorth never asks for bank logins and doesn't connect to any financial service.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the new iPhone with the same Apple ID and install myWorth. Your data downloads from iCloud. Other iPhones signed in to the same Apple ID stay in sync too.

How do I share with my partner?

Go to Settings and tap Invite Partner, then send the invite by Messages or another way. Your partner needs an iPhone with myWorth installed and an Apple ID signed in to iCloud. Once they join, you both see and edit the same accounts, and each of you can change your own name and icon.

If you'd rather not share, tap Add Partner Without Inviting. You can then assign accounts to your partner and see totals for each of you, on your iPhone only.

What happens to my accounts when I join my partner's household?

myWorth tells you how many accounts you have and asks before joining. Your accounts move into the shared household, keep who they belong to, and your partner can see and edit them. If you leave later, you keep the accounts assigned to you.

How do we stop sharing?

Either of you can do it from Settings: tap Leave Household if you joined, or Remove [partner] if you sent the invite. Each of you keeps the accounts assigned to you. Joint accounts stay with the person who sent the invite. This finishes the next time the other iPhone opens myWorth. If that iPhone can't open the app, the person who sent the invite can tap Remove Now, but the other person then loses the accounts assigned to them.

Changes aren't showing up on another iPhone. What should I check?

  • Both iPhones are connected to the internet and signed in to iCloud.
  • iCloud is turned on for myWorth in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • Your iCloud storage isn't full.

Changes usually arrive within a minute, but can take longer. Opening myWorth often helps it catch up.

Why does myWorth ask for Face ID every time?

myWorth locks whenever you leave the app, so someone picking up your iPhone can't open it straight to your finances. Unlock with Face ID or Touch ID, or tap Use Passcode. If your iPhone has no passcode set, myWorth can't lock.

Which currency does myWorth use?

The currency of your iPhone's region, set in Settings → General → Language & Region. myWorth doesn't convert between currencies.

Does deleting the app delete my data?

No. It removes myWorth's data from that iPhone only. Your data stays in your iCloud and comes back if you reinstall. If you share a household, you stay in it until one of you stops sharing.

How do I delete all my data?

In Settings, tap Erase All Data and type “Erase” to confirm. This deletes everything myWorth stores on your iPhone and in your iCloud, including names, and can't be undone. If you shared your household, your partner loses access to it too, including the accounts assigned to them. If you joined your partner's household, you leave it without keeping any accounts.

Privacy

myWorth doesn't collect any data. Your data stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. Read the full myWorth Privacy Policy.

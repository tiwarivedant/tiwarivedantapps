myWallet Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · Vedant Tiwari

In short: myWallet doesn't collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. myWallet makes no network requests. It has no server, no accounts or sign-in, and no iCloud sync, so nothing you enter is ever sent to me or anyone else.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device database:

  • The expenses you log (amount, category, date, and an optional merchant and note)
  • Your categories and monthly budgets
  • One app setting that remembers the default categories were already created

This data never leaves your device through the app. Like any iOS app, its data is included in your own iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Apple handles those backups, not me.

Third-party services

None. myWallet has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party SDKs. No data is shared or sold, and you are not tracked.

Children's privacy

myWallet collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app from your iPhone. That permanently deletes all of its data. There's no copy anywhere else, so I can't recover it.

This website

This site uses no cookies or analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
